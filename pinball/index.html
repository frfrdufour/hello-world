How to score

  • Two ramps, the Staircase and the Belfry. Alternate them for combos. Three ramps relight the kickback.
  • R-I-P targets open the Crypt. Shoot it to explore a room.
  • Six rooms light the manor windows. Explore all six for Midnight Madness.
  • B-A-T targets light the lock. Lock three balls for Ghost Multiball.
  • B-O-O lanes raise your bonus multiplier. Flippers shift the lit lanes.
  • Hit Grimble the gargoyle. He hates it.
